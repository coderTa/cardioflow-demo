CardioFlow

Start from your own data

Export your Apple Health archive and drop it here. The simulator will open on a subject built from your own measurements instead of from a population average.

How to export it

  1. Open the Health app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your picture in the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Export All Health Data.
  4. Save or send the zip to this computer, then drop it below.

What gets read

Date of birth, sex, height, weight, resting heart rate, blood pressure if a cuff has written any, exercise minutes, sleep, and aerobic capacity. About twenty numbers in all.

What leaves this computer

Nothing. The zip is opened and scanned here, in your browser. It is never uploaded, and closing this tab is the end of it.

Your archive holds far more than the list above: cycles, medications, mindfulness, and your clinical records if you have connected a health system. None of it is read, and none of it goes anywhere.

Drop your export.zip here

It stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Your measurements are real. The subject built from them is not you: everything this model does not contain is still missing, and the parts your archive could not fill are population averages wearing your heart rate.
