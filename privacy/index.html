What is kept

If you import an Apple Health archive, this is everything that happens to it. It is short because there is not much to say.

Your archive never leaves your computer

The zip is opened and read in your own browser. It is not uploaded, not streamed, and not sent anywhere at any point. Nothing on any server ever sees it. When you close the tab, it is over.

That matters because a Health export is not a file of heart rates. It holds everything Health has ever recorded about you: cycles, medications, mindfulness minutes, sexual activity, and your clinical records if you have connected a health system. This simulator reads eight kinds of measurement out of it and ignores the rest.

What can be stored, if you ask for it

Only the derived summary: about two dozen numbers, which are the ones the simulation actually runs on. Your age, sex, height, weight, resting heart rate, sleep, exercise minutes, and, if your archive has them, blood pressure and aerobic capacity.

It is stored only if you sign in and ask. Without an account, nothing is stored at all and every import lasts as long as the tab.

How it is stored

  • Encrypted before it is written, with AES-256-GCM, using a key that is held separately from everything else. A copy of the database is a pile of ciphertext.
  • Filed under a one-way identifier derived from your email address, not under the address. Your address is not stored anywhere, so a copy of the database cannot be turned back into a list of people.
  • Deleted by itself 90 days after the last time you saved it. That is the same window the measurements themselves cover: a summary older than the data behind it describes somebody who has moved on.

Signing in

There is no password, so there is no password here to lose. You give an email address, a link is sent to it, and following the link sets a cookie in that browser. The link works once and stops working after fifteen minutes. The cookie lasts a month and cannot be read by any script on the page.

Deleting

The delete control on your account removes the record. There is no flag, no tombstone, and nothing to restore. It is gone.

Signing out is a different thing and does not delete anything. It clears the cookie in that browser and leaves the summary where it is until it expires or you delete it.

What is not here

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts, and nothing is sold or shared with anybody. Your measurements are not used to improve anything, train anything, or compare you with anyone.

And the thing this page cannot fix

A simulated subject built from your measurements is still a simulation. It has no genetics, no family history, no imaging, and no way to know the state of your coronary arteries, which is the single largest thing that decides a real heart’s future. Nothing here is a medical device, an assessment of you, or a reason to act or not act.

The assumption register lists every simplification the model makes, in full.
