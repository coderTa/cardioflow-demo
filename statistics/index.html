The list above moves one input at a time with everything else held. That is the easiest sensitivity analysis to compute and the easiest to over-trust, because it is blind to interactions by construction, and the largest effects in this model are interactions.

Look at the surface. A 60 % stenosis at rest is almost invisible; ten METs with clean arteries is almost invisible; together they fall off a cliff. Neither axis on its own would have told you the cliff was there. Read the ranking as a list of candidates and the surface as the answer.
