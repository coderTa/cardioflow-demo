Your account

An account does one thing: it remembers the subject you imported, so you do not have to export your Health archive again on another device.

What is never stored

  • Your Health archive. It is opened and read on your own computer and never uploaded at all, so there is nothing here to keep.
  • Your email address. Only a one-way identifier derived from it.
  • Anything readable. The summary is encrypted before it is written, with a key held apart from everything else.

Educational simulation built on simplified physiological models. It is not a medical device, and nothing it reports is medical advice or a validated prediction.
